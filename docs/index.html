How to connect your wallet (owner only)
  1. Use a browser wallet such as MetaMask or Rabby, holding the key that signs for the owner's smart account (the Kernel account that owns PayRun).
  2. Press Connect wallet. If your wallet is on another network, the page asks it to switch to (or add) Aevum testnet: chain ID 44005, RPC https://rpc-test.qday.info, currency tQDAY, explorer https://explorer-test.qday.info.
  3. The top bar then says whether this key is the owner's signer. Any other key can look, but its approvals fail on chain.
  4. Approve and pay, Confirm payee or Reject: your wallet asks you to sign one UserOperation for the smart account, then to send it (a little tQDAY for gas). Each result links to the explorer.
  5. A held payment: first call the vendor on a number you already had, tick the box, then Confirm payee. After that the run can be approved and paid.

The page never holds a key and only sends to Clerk's two PayRun contracts. Without a wallet it is read-only.
